Frequently asked questions

I bought the Arcade Pack but the games are locked.

Open the App, go to the Settings tab and tap Restore Purchases under Arcade Pack. Make sure the device is signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

Can my kids use the Arcade Pack on their own devices?

Yes. The Arcade Pack supports Family Sharing. Once purchase sharing is turned on in your Family group, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases on each family member's device.

Will I have to pay again for new games?

No. The Arcade Pack is a one-time purchase that covers every game in the App, including every game we release in the future. Once you own it, you have access to all games forever, with nothing more to buy.

Is it really free of ads?

Yes. There are no ads anywhere in the App, in the free game or the paid ones. There are also no "watch a video to continue" prompts, no pop-ups, and no coins or gems for sale. The Arcade Pack is the only purchase, and it is a one-time unlock.

Does the App need internet?

No. Every game works fully offline. The only network use is Apple's App Store when you buy or restore the Arcade Pack.

How do I turn off the sound?

Tap the speaker button on the main menu or on any game's landing page. One switch silences music and effects across the whole App.

Where are my high scores and drawings kept?

On your device only. Nothing is uploaded. If you delete the App, its saved data is deleted with it, so back up any drawings you want to keep by saving them to Photos from inside Coloring.

What data does the App collect?

None. No analytics, no accounts, no tracking. See the Privacy Policy for the details.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running a current version of iOS or iPadOS. Check the App Store listing for the exact minimum version.

I found a bug or the App crashed.

Sorry about that. Email us with your device model, iOS version, and which game you were playing.
