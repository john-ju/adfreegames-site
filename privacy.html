Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

The short version: Ad-Free Games collects nothing. No personal information, no usage data, no ads, no tracking. Everything the app saves stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy describes how One Light Media LLC ("we", "us") handles information in the Ad-Free Games app for iPhone and iPad (the "App") and on this website. The App is designed to be completely private, especially for children.

What we collect

Nothing. The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data of any kind.

  • The App works entirely offline. Its only network use is Apple's App Store (StoreKit) for the optional Arcade Pack purchase, which Apple processes. The App never sees your payment details or identity.
  • There are no ads and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • There are no accounts, logins, or sign-ups.
  • The one in-app purchase is a one-time unlock. There are no subscriptions and no consumable purchases. It covers every game in the App, including future ones, runs through the App Store's own purchase confirmation, and supports Family Sharing.
  • The only things the App saves are your high scores and progress, chosen difficulties and colors, unlocked ships and boosters, your sound preference, and, in Coloring, the drawings you make. All of it is stored locally on your device only and is never sent anywhere. Deleting the App deletes it.

Camera and photos

Coloring can turn a photo into a coloring page. If you tap "Use a Photo", the App uses Apple's photo picker, which does not give the App access to your photo library. If you choose to take a picture instead, the App asks for camera permission. Saving a finished drawing asks for add-only permission to your photo library. Photos and drawings never leave the device.

Children's privacy

The App is rated 4+ and made for kids. Because it collects no data and has no ads, no third-party network access, and no external links inside the games, it is suitable for children and is designed to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and similar laws. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Purchases

The Arcade Pack is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We receive only aggregate, anonymous sales reports from Apple and cannot identify individual buyers.

This website

adfreegames.app is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts on it, and we do not set cookies. GitHub may log standard server information such as IP addresses to operate the service, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Because the App collects no data, we do not expect it to change in any way that affects you.
