Questions parents ask

Short answers to the things people ask before handing the app to a kid.

Are there really no ads?

None. No banners, no pop-ups, no "watch a video to continue", not in the free game and not in the paid ones. The app has no ad SDKs in it at all.

Is it a subscription?

No. The app is free to download and Hole is free to play. The Arcade Pack is a single one-time purchase through Apple ($4.99 in the US App Store; prices vary by country) that unlocks every other game, including every game we add in the future. There is nothing else to buy: no coins, no gems, no monthly fee.

Which game is free?

Hole, the 3D swallow-the-city game, is free to play with no ads and no time limits. The other fourteen games are in the Arcade Pack.

What ages is it for?

Rated 4+ and designed for young kids: big buttons, no reading needed to start a game, and Chill modes in the faster games for little ones. Older kids and grown-ups play it too, especially Mancala, Connect 4, Gem Swap and Block Burst.

Does it work offline, on a plane?

Yes. Every game works fully offline. The only time the app uses the internet is Apple's App Store when you buy or restore the Arcade Pack.

Does it collect any data or need an account?

No. No accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no tracking. Scores and drawings stay on the device. See the privacy policy.

Can my kids use the Arcade Pack on their own iPads?

Yes. The Arcade Pack supports Family Sharing. Buy it once, turn on purchase sharing in your Family group, then tap Restore Purchases in the app's Settings tab on each device.

Can my child buy things inside the games?

No. There is no in-game currency for sale and nothing to buy inside any game. The only purchase in the whole app is the Arcade Pack, made through Apple, so Ask to Buy and Screen Time limits apply.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Ad-Free Games is for iPhone and iPad.
